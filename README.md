One image summing up the world news of **Wednesday, 25 November 2026** —
a newspaper front page drawn by `main.py` (pure Python standard library,
no network at run time).

- **Lead story:** the CIA and the Office of the Director of National
  Intelligence hit by a cyber breach exposing unclassified information
  (reported by the New York Times, confirmed by the Associated Press).
- **46 stories, 8 sections:** North America, Europe, Middle East,
  Asia-Pacific, Africa, Latin America, Markets & Tech, World & Science.
- Every story was verified live from the 25 November 2026 morning news
  cycle (Reuters, AP, NYT, CNN, RFE/RL, Xinhua, WHO, WFP). Items whose
  event date was provably earlier were discarded.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # writes out/entry.png (1024x1024)
```

Two runs write byte-identical PNGs (589,253 bytes, deterministic).
